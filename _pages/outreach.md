**Volunteer** · Apr. 2024 · Marion, IL

Conducted solar observing for 6 hours on the day of the total solar eclipse at the Marion Baseball Stadium for around 1,500 people of all ages with a team of 13 volunteers, while answering questions about stellar astrophysics and solar physics.
